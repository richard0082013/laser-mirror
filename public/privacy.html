隐私说明

存在你设备上的内容

进度、金币、道具、每日题成绩、语言和音效设置都保存在你的浏览器里（localStorage），不会上传到我们这里。清除浏览器数据后，这些记录会消失。

匿名统计

如果这个版本开启了统计，游戏会向 GoatCounter 发送事件名称，比如“过了某一关”“复制了成绩”“今天是第几天回来”。我们不发送玩家编号，也不使用 Cookie。发送请求时，统计服务会收到浏览器通常都会发送的信息，比如 IP 地址和浏览器类型，具体处理方式见它的隐私政策。

你可以在商店底部的设置里关闭统计。浏览器开启“请勿跟踪”时，游戏也不会发送。

分享和挑战链接

你复制的成绩里只有期号和步数，只有你自己发给别人时才会被看到。

Privacy

Stored on your device

Your progress, coins, items, daily results, language and sound settings stay in your browser (localStorage). They are not uploaded to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Anonymous stats

If stats are enabled in this version, the game sends event names to GoatCounter, such as “cleared a level”, “copied a result” or “which day since first visit”. We send no player ID and use no cookies. Like any web request, the service receives information your browser normally sends, such as IP address and browser type. See its privacy policy for how it handles this.

You can turn stats off in the settings at the bottom of the shop. The game also sends nothing when your browser has Do Not Track on.

Sharing and challenge links

A copied result contains only the puzzle number and your step count. Others see it only if you send it to them.
